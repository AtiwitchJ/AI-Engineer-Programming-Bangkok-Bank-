Company International Travel Policy : Employees who need to travel abroad for business must obtain approval from their department manager at least 14 business days in advance and submit a Travel Request form stating the purpose, itinerary, and estimated expenses. The company covers economy-class airfare, accommodation within the approved rate, and a daily allowance based on the destination country rate table. Personal side trips during a business trip must be declared in advance and are not reimbursable. Employees must submit a travel summary report within 7 days after returning.

SmartSave Savings Account Product : The SmartSave savings account can be opened with a minimum deposit of 500 THB. It pays interest at 1.25% per year, calculated daily and credited at the end of each month. There is no annual fee, and withdrawals are unlimited through the mobile app or the bank's ATMs. Customers can set savings goals and receive alerts when the balance reaches the target. This account is suitable for short- to medium-term savers who want convenient access to their funds.

General Facts About Domestic Cats : Domestic cats are popular pets that sleep an average of 12 to 16 hours per day. Their hearing is much better than that of humans, especially for high-frequency sounds. Cats communicate through vocalizations, tail movements, and purring, which often signals contentment or relaxation. Most cats prefer a territory they consider their own and may become stressed when their environment changes suddenly.
